Pocket Lanes

The privacy policy for the bowling game for iPhone and iPhone Duo.

Support Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Pocket Lanes is made by Silas Beam.

What I collect: nothing

The game has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of mine. It sends me no data about you or how you play.

What stays on your iPhone

Your scores, settings and progress. They are stored by the game on your phone and are deleted when you delete the game.

What Apple handles

If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple receives your scores and achievements and shows your Game Center name on leaderboards. Purchases go through Apple's App Store; I see sales totals, never who you are or how you paid. Apple's own privacy policy covers these services.

Playing with a friend

When you play on two phones, your throws, scores, chosen ball and lane, level and first name or Game Center name go to your friend's phone through Apple's SharePlay or Game Center, and theirs come to yours. If you play inside Messages, your score travels in the message you send. None of it passes through a server of mine.

Pictures and clips you choose to share

A score card or a clip goes only where you send it from the share sheet. The game asks to add to your photos only when you choose to save one, and it never reads your photos. If you turn on Save clips, iOS keeps the last few seconds of the game's screen in memory on your phone; a clip is made only when you tap Clip. The game never records sound.

Age

Pocket Lanes is rated 16+ on the App Store and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete. Deleting the game removes what it stored on your phone. Game Center data is managed in your iPhone's Game Center settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions

Write to pocketlanes@icloud.com.
